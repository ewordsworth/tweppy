How this works

X removed likes and quote posts from its paid API in April 2026, and only accepts an API reply when the other person mentioned or quoted you. So Like, Reply and Quote open X with everything filled in, and you confirm there. Use a browser where you are signed in to X.

Replies to posts that mention you can be sent from here. Checking costs about ${{ settings.cost_per_read }} per post read.
